## Long description (4000 char)

### English

ÆTERNA is a personal biology operating system. It connects the signals your
body gives — sleep, recovery, labs, weight, training, mood — and shows you
what actually changed when you started a protocol.

Most apps track what you do. ÆTERNA helps you understand what it does to
you.

### What you get

**Protocol → Response**
Start a protocol and ÆTERNA tracks the downstream changes — sleep, HRV,
resting heart rate, weight, waist, labs. After a few weeks you see the
response:
"Since starting MOTS-c: recovery ↑14%, sleep +22 min, resting HR −3 bpm.
Strongest signal: recovery."

**n=1 Experiments**
Formalize a hypothesis, define a primary metric, run it for N weeks, and
let ÆTERNA score the outcome against your baseline. Your own personal
biology laboratory.

**Compound library**
174 peptides and compounds with monographs, reconstitution math, half-life
charts, and an interaction checker. All bundled — works offline.

**Protocol builder & templates**
Structured protocol builder with versioning. Ready-made templates
(Wolverine stack, healing, GLP-1 reset, anti-aging, cognitive, sleep) you
can adapt to yourself.

**Dose & injection tracking**
Log doses and injections, rotate sites intelligently, track vial inventory.

**Labs & biomarkers**
Import your blood work (PDF or CSV), see longitudinal trends per marker,
and watch which markers moved after a protocol.

**Biology timeline**
Protocols, labs, symptoms, dose changes, and biological events on one
chronological axis — your body's history, finally legible.

**Stack intelligence**
See whether your stack skews recovery, metabolic, cognitive, sleep, or
longevity. Get a heads-up when compounds overlap on the same pathway.

**AEON — your biology's AI**
Ask AEON "why have I been more tired lately?" and it reads your data:
"Your sleep dropped 48 min and training load rose 31% in the last two
weeks — those are the strongest variables associated with fatigue."
Bring your own AI key, or self-host. Never ambient.

**Nutrition & training**
7,000+ offline foods (USDA) and 870+ exercises (wger). Full logging
without a server round-trip.

### Private by design

• No ads. No analytics. No tracking. Verified by code audit.
• Works fully offline in "local-only" mode — no account required.
• Point it at your own server, the reference server, or none.
• Your data is yours: export, delete, or move anytime.

### Free, and staying that way

No paywall. No subscription. No "premium" tier hiding the useful features.
No ads. No selling data. Everyone gets everything.

### Not medical advice

ÆTERNA is educational software. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or
prevent any disease. It does not prescribe or recommend any compound or
dose. It does not claim a protocol *caused* a change — only that the
change coincided in time. Always consult a qualified healthcare
professional before starting, changing, or stopping any compound,
supplement, or medication.
